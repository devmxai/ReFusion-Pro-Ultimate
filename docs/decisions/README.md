# Decision system

- RFCs explore unresolved high-impact changes and expire at a decision date.
- ADRs record accepted or rejected decisions and their consequences.
- An accepted ADR is historical evidence; supersede it with a new ADR rather
  than rewriting the decision.
- `REGISTER.md` will be generated from document front matter and is not a
  second decision source.

Agents may draft a decision but may not silently approve product, legal,
security, or architecture authority on behalf of its owner.
